At **Liberty Camp Moldova 2026**, participants will engage with international instructors and like-minded young people from different countries while exploring topics such as:

- Entrepreneurship
- Classical Liberal Philosophy
- Leadership
- Individual Liberty and Human Flourishing

Whether you're passionate about ideas, looking to develop practical skills, or eager to connect with a global network of freedom-minded students and young professionals, this camp is for you.

The camp runs for five days at the Tipova Nature Reserve. The registration fee is 15 euro and includes accommodation, meals, and transport between Chișinău and Horodiște. Details and registration: [liveandletlive.org/llichisinau](https://liveandletlive.org/llichisinau).
